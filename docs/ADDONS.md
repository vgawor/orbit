# Orbit add-ons: the plan

Orbit is a set of small apps (modules), integrations and Home widgets that live in the notch.
This plan grows it into seven categories. Each has 20 ideas; the **5 in bold are built** (in the
app under `Viktor/Modules/AddOns/<Category>/`, added from **Settings → Add-ons**).

Tier: **F** = Free, **P** = Pro. AI add-ons use the person's own Claude key.

## How Free and Pro work

**Orbit Free.** The app and every Free add-on, with no time limit and no account: the notch,
music, meetings, tasks (with HubSpot), focus, notes, shelf, presenting, Agents, and bots and
Smart Ask with your own Claude or ChatGPT key. Free has to be genuinely useful, because it's how
people find Orbit.

**Orbit Pro, $12 a month.** An optional account that adds:
- bots that run on Orbit's AI keys, with AI use included, so you don't need your own API account;
- approving bots from your phone, with notifications;
- every Pro add-on, today's and future ones.

**How it's enforced in the app.** `AddOnInfo.tier` marks each add-on. `Entitlements.isPro`
follows the Orbit account's plan, and Settings → Add-ons shows a Pro badge and an Upgrade button.
